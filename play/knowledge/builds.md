# Standard builds (general game knowledge `[wiki]`)

Strong, simple single-class builds to level 12, for planning party-build.md. Patch details differ: confirm
names with `get_level_up_options` before writing a plan line. Feats/ASIs at 4/8/12 (Fighter +6, Rogue +10).
"+1/+1" = Ability Improvement split, e.g. `{"Wisdom":1,"Constitution":1}`. Odd main stat at start → split
(17 → +1 main +1 CON), even → +2 main. Main stat to 20 first unless a key feat is listed.
Good feats in general: Great Weapon Master / Sharpshooter (martial damage), War Caster (concentration
casters), Alert (initiative, never surprised), Tough, Polearm Master, Lucky, Resilient (CON) for casters.

## Paladin: Oath of Devotion (the default PC without a character brief) — tank, smiter, face
- Stats: STR > CHA > CON. Fighting style (L2): Great Weapon Fighting (two-handed) or Defence (shield).
- L4 Great Weapon Master (two-handed) or +2 STR · L8 +2 STR · L12 +2 CHA (Aura of Protection adds CHA to saves).
- Key: Extra Attack L5, Aura of Protection L6; Divine Smite on crits and bosses; spells Bless, Shield of
  Faith, Command, Compelled Duel (L2-4), Aid, Magic Weapon (L5+), Revivify (L9+).
- **Keep the oath:** the Paladin landing the killing blow on someone who was not an enemy breaks Devotion (S026: a
  confused Cloudkill killed a patient; S028: the bow finished the Warden, "Temporarily Hostile" after being caught
  in the Moonrise prison). Restoring it at the camp's Oathbreaker Knight cost 1000 g the first time and 2000 g the
  second. When guards/neutrals turn temporarily hostile, let companions take the kills (or knock them out), and
  keep the Paladin on the real enemies.
## Cleric: Trickery (Shadowheart) / Life — healer, support
- Stats: WIS > CON > STR/DEX (medium armour + shield). Shadowheart starts WIS 17 → L4 +1 WIS +1 CON.
- L4 +1/+1 · L8 +2 WIS · L12 War Caster or Alert.
- Spells: Bless, Healing Word, Guiding Bolt, Sanctuary · L3 Spiritual Weapon, Aid, Hold Person ·
  L5 Spirit Guardians (the core spell), Mass Healing Word, Revivify · L7 Banishment, Guardian of Faith ·
  L9 Mass Cure Wounds, Insect Plague · L11 Heal, Heroes' Feast. Trickery: Blessing of the Trickster,
  Invoke Duplicity (L2). Life (if a new cleric): bigger heals, heavy armour.
## Wizard: Evocation (Gale) — area damage, control
- School at L2: **Evocation** (Sculpt Spells: your area spells spare allies — best for an AI that fireballs
  near its own party) or Divination (Portent dice). Stats: INT > CON > DEX.
- L4 +1 INT +1 CON (Gale INT 17) · L8 +2 INT · L12 War Caster or Alert.
- Learn: L1-2 Magic Missile, Shield, Sleep, Thunderwave, Grease, Find Familiar · L3-4 Misty Step,
  Scorching Ray, Hold Person, Web, Mirror Image, Cloud of Daggers · L5-6 Fireball, Haste, Counterspell,
  Hypnotic Pattern · L7-8 Greater Invisibility, Banishment, Ice Storm, Dimension Door · L9-10 Cone of
  Cold, Hold Monster, Bigby's Hand · L11-12 Chain Lightning, Globe of Invulnerability, Disintegrate.
  Copy scrolls into the spellbook when found.
## Fighter: Battle Master (Lae'zel) — frontline damage, control
- Subclass L3 Battle Master (manoeuvres: Trip Attack, Menacing Attack, Precision Attack, Riposte,
  Pushing Attack). Stats: STR > CON. Style: Great Weapon Fighting.
- L4 Great Weapon Master · L6 +2 STR · L8 +2 STR / Polearm Master · L12 Savage Attacker or Alert.
- Extra Attack L5, a third attack L11; Action Surge for burst turns.
## Barbarian: Berserker or Wildheart (Karlach) — frontline tank
- Subclass L3: Berserker (Frenzy: a bonus-action attack while raging) or Wildheart Bear (resist all
  but psychic while raging). Stats: STR > CON, no armour needed (Unarmoured Defence).
- L4 Great Weapon Master · L8 +2 STR · L12 +2 CON or Savage Attacker. Rage before the first swing; Reckless Attack.
## Rogue: Thief or Assassin (Astarion) — skills, locks, traps, ranged
- Subclass L3: Thief (Fast Hands: two bonus actions) or Assassin (crits on surprised). Stats: DEX > CON/WIS.
- L4 Sharpshooter (bow) or +2 DEX · L8 +2 DEX · L10 Alert · L12 Lucky. Expertise: Sleight of Hand,
  Perception (or Stealth). Cunning Action Hide/Disengage; keep him on lockpicks and trap disarming.
## Warlock: Fiend (Wyll) — ranged damage
- Patron at L1. Invocations L2 Agonizing Blast + Repelling Blast; Pact (L3) of the Blade (melee) or
  Tome. Stats: CHA > CON/DEX. L4 +2 CHA · L8 +2 CHA · L12 War Caster / Alert.
- Eldritch Blast every turn; Hex, Hold Person, Hunger of Hadar, Counterspell, Hypnotic Pattern.
## Druid: Circle of the Moon (Halsin) / Land (Jaheira) — tank shapes or caster (Act 2)
- Subclass L2. WIS > CON. L4 +2 WIS (or War Caster for Land) · L8 +2 WIS · L12 Alert.
- Moon: wild shapes as a tank (Owlbear, later elementals). Land: Spike Growth, Moonbeam, Call Lightning,
  Conjure Elemental; Healing Word for support.
## Ranger: Gloom Stalker or Hunter (Minsc, Act 3) — ranged/skills
- Subclass L3. DEX > WIS. L4 Sharpshooter · L8 +2 DEX · L12 Alert. Hunter's Mark, Ensnaring Strike.
## Bard: Lore or Swords — support, face, skills
- Subclass L3 (Lore: Cutting Words, extra skills). CHA > DEX/CON. L4 +2 CHA · L8 +2 CHA · L12 War Caster.
- Healing Word, Faerie Fire, Hypnotic Pattern, Hold Person, Dimension Door; Bardic Inspiration on allies.
## Sorcerer: Draconic or Storm — area damage
- Subclass L1. CHA > CON. L4 +2 CHA · L8 +2 CHA · L12 War Caster. Twinned Haste/Hold, Quickened Fireball.
## Monk: Open Hand — mobile striker
- Subclass L3. DEX > WIS. L4 +2 DEX · L8 +2 DEX · L12 Alert/Tavern Brawler. Flurry of Blows, Stunning Strike.

## A balanced Act 1 party from the current one
Your PC (default Paladin: front + face) · Shadowheart (Cleric, heals + Spirit Guardians) · Gale (Evocation
Wizard, Sleep/Web/Fireball) · **Astarion** (Rogue: locks, traps, ranged, stealth). Swap in Lae'zel or
