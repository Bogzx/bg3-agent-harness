# Finishing checklist (spoiler-light)

Read this at session start, before crossing any region boundary, and before every long rest.
It doesn't cover the story. It lists what can **end the run, lock content, or fail a quest
without warning**. For details, use `wiki_search` / `wiki_page`. Everything here counts as
`[wiki]` knowledge until you see it in game.

Turn limits below are for **Explorer** difficulty; Balanced and harder give fewer turns.

---

## Always

**Never pick these** (each can end the game on the spot):
- Giving in to a mind flayer, a hag, a devil or a god: options like "submit", "surrender", "lean in",
  "give up your body/mind", "let it take you". Refuse, resist, or leave.
- Mocking or challenging a god-like being to prove itself.
- Refusing the devil's final offer in the Astral Plane **unless the party holds the Orphic Hammer**.
- Attacking your mysterious protector outside dialogue in the Astral Plane, or letting it die in the
  fight where you defend it.
- Activating Gale's orb in Act 2 or 3 (unless that's the deliberate final-sacrifice choice).
- Letting a companion finish what they start on you during a night at camp, or surrendering to one in
  an early confrontation. These are only deadly when the party is just the PC and that companion, so
  keep at least three in the party.
- Jumping into a deep hole or chasm with **every** party member at once and no fall protection
  (Feather Fall, levitation, a fall-proof item).

**Points of no return** (the game warns you; treat every warning as a stop sign):

| Crossing | What closes |
|---|---|
| First steps onto the Rosymorn Monastery trail (Mountain Pass) | Much of the Druid Grove and Waukeen's Rest content resolves |
| Entering the Shadow-Cursed Lands | Same as above; Act 2 begins |
| Entering the Shadowfell (bottom of the Gauntlet of Shar) | **All of Act 1** (Wilderness, Underdark) becomes unreachable |
| Leaving Act 2 for Baldur's Gate | All of Act 2 |
| Entering the Morphic Pool | Camp, resting, the stash, party swaps and respec. This is the final sequence |

Before each crossing: hard save, clear the "Before you cross" list for the act, and record the crossing in
decisions.md.

**Before every long rest**, check for anything that ticks with rests. If one of these is open, deal with it
first:
- A party member died and their revival quest is open (Gale: **3 rests** and the game ends).
- Someone is poisoned or trapped, or a timed rescue is open (a poisoned gnome in the Underdark, a duergar
  prisoner behind rubble in Grymforge, a counsellor facing execution in Baldur's Gate, a murder that's
  about to happen).
- A camp scene the journal asks for ("celebrate", "rest and reflect"). **Those** you should rest for.

**Fights you shouldn't take:** if something is clearly far above your level, or you're out of slots and
potions, leave, rest and come back. Exception: fights that the story or a turn counter forces.

## Prologue: the nautiloid

- Mostly linear. Recruit the githyanki warrior and the cleric in the pod when offered.
- When a **turn counter** appears ("reach the helm"), stop looting and move straight to the helm
  console. You have 15 turns. Don't leave the room while that fight is going, and don't go back to the
  healing pod once it's over.

## Act 1: Wilderness, Underdark, Grymforge, Rosymorn

**Goal:** find a cure for the tadpole. Get the druid Halsin's help (or the alternative leader's), then
head for Moonrise Towers.

**Recruit early:** the cleric on the beach (before going to camp or the Grove), the rogue near the crash,
the wizard at the waystone, the warrior caged near the Grove road.

**Route sketch:** crash site → Druid (Emerald) Grove → Blighted Village → Goblin Camp (Shattered Sanctum)
→ deal with its three leaders → long rest at camp (celebration) → talk to Halsin → Mountain Pass **or**
Underdark → Shadow-Cursed Lands.

**Before you cross into Act 2** (Rosymorn trail or Shadow-Cursed Lands):
- [ ] Resolve the Grove dispute (the ritual / the tiefling refugees). Otherwise the Grove seals and the
      refugees are lost.
- [ ] Free Halsin from the goblins before leaving.
- [ ] Have the post-victory celebration long rest at camp, and talk to everyone.
- [ ] Hard save.

**Time-sensitive things (rest, fast travel or leaving the area):**
- Waukeen's Rest fire: act immediately on arrival. Resting, fast travel or leaving lets the trapped people die.
- A poisoned gnome in the Underdark: 3 rests.
- A duergar prisoner behind rubble in Grymforge, and the gnome slaves: 2 rests or 2 departures.
- Lae'zel's githyanki patrol encounter: speak to their captain before you fast travel or rest.

**Hazards:**
- Grymforge: a gnome threatens to blow a powder barrel. Keep **at least one** party member outside the
  room (more than 18 m away) when you talk her down.
- The Blood of Lathander vault (Rosymorn) has a 4-round escape trap. Have someone stand outside, or find the
  crest that disarms it first.
- The temple under the Goblin Camp has a moon-tile puzzle. Moves are unlimited; rotate until the dark moons
  sit on the lit disc. A hidden lever (Perception, then lockpick) bypasses it. Optional.
- A creepy book in the village cellar: optional, and its saves inflict curses. **Skip it.**

## Act 2: Shadow-Cursed Lands

**Goal:** find the Nightsong, reach Ketheric Thorm at Moonrise Towers, and go below the Towers.

**The curse:** away from magical light, the party takes stacking curse damage. Move between light sources
(Last Light Inn, moonlanterns, braziers) or carry a protective blessing. Reach the Last Light Inn first.

**Route sketch:** Ruined Battlefield → Last Light Inn → Reithwin Town / Grand Mausoleum → Moonrise Towers
(get in as a "True Soul" while that still works) → Gauntlet of Shar → Nightsong → assault on Moonrise →
the Ketheric fights → the colony below.

**Before entering the Shadowfell:**
- [ ] Finish anything you still want from Act 1 (it closes for good).
- [ ] Visit Moonrise Towers at least once.
- [ ] Rescue and escort quests near Moonrise and Last Light done, or deliberately abandoned.
- [ ] Hard save.

**Gauntlet of Shar, one-line hints:**
- Entrance gem door: put out **every** light in the room, including spells and lanterns, then walk the
  gaps in the glyph rows. A teleport past the door also works.
- Stealth trial: shadows with darkvision patrol it. Kill one in a single hit or sneak; getting spotted
  resets you. One character finishing is enough.
- Mirror trial: unequip gear before entering so the copies spawn weak. Only fight your own copy.
- Leap trial: the safe path is drawn on the floor mosaic before the bowl. Send one character; the third
  misstep kills.
- Gem lifts: ride down in small groups.
- Library: **don't destroy** the book about the Nightsinger. Disarm the bookcase traps before looting.
- A big mercenary in the gauntlet has a contract with a hidden clause. Read it with Insight.

**Timers:**
- Defending Halsin's portal while he lifts the curse: 4 turns.
- The colony and final Ketheric fights are long. Rest before going down.

## Act 3: Rivington, Wyrm's Crossing, Lower City

**Goal:** get two Netherstones (from Gortash and from Orin), gather allies, then the final assault.

**Recommended order:**
1. Rivington → Wyrm's Crossing → **attend Gortash's coronation** at Wyrm's Rock. Don't attack yet.
2. Lower City. Speak with Mizora (camp) and the two Flaming Fist officers in camp **before** the Iron
   Throne trip.
3. **Iron Throne before the Steel Watch Foundry.** Destroying the Foundry first makes the Iron Throne
   unreachable.
4. Destroy the Steel Watch Foundry.
5. Then fight Gortash. With the Watch disabled, it's far easier. The throne room has traps; watch your
   footing.
6. Orin in the Temple of Bhaal. Expect a companion to be taken. Follow that lead.
7. Gather Your Allies: recruit every faction you can. They help in the final battle.

**Iron Throne (timed):** 8 turns from when the countdown starts. Before stepping out of the submarine, buff
up in turn-based mode (Haste, Longstrider). Open the farthest cells first. Ranged attacks and Magic Missile
can open doors and levers from a distance. Everyone must be back in the sluice chamber when the counter
ends, or they die. If nobody makes it back, the game is over. Never use the submarine controls to leave early.

**Deadlines:**
- Execution notice for the counsellor: 5 rests.
- The tribunal murder: 1 rest after you learn of it.
- The newspaper hit piece: 1 rest.

## Endgame (Morphic Pool onward)

**Before entering the Morphic Pool:**
- [ ] Both Netherstones in hand, and Orin and Gortash dealt with.
- [ ] Every ally faction you want is recruited, and companion business you care about is done.
- [ ] Either hold the **Orphic Hammer** or accept that you'll need the devil's deal or the Emperor's side.
- [ ] Full long rest, potions and scrolls distributed, gear final. You can't come back.
- [ ] Hard save named `…-pre-morphic`.

**Inside:**
- Expect repeated Constitution saves, an ambush, and a mind contest using the Netherstones. Losing that
  contest is a Game Over, so the normal reload rule applies.
- The party is forced to choose a side. See the never-pick list.
- Upper city and High Hall: allies can be summoned with a special action. Use it.
- The final brain has **turn counters**. When one appears, stop everything else and put all damage on the
  target the objective names.

## If something goes wrong

- Game Over screen: load the latest save. If it was taken mid-disaster, load the previous hard save.
- A quest won't advance: check the journal text, then `wiki_page("<quest name>")`. Long resting
  sometimes advances it (unless a deadline above applies).
- No way forward found after three different tries: run the `stuck` skill.
